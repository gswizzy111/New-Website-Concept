# Chosen direction (Nov 16, 2026)

The owner dropped the pixel / healing-center concept ("scratch the idea") and
asked for a site that looks amazing and photo-led, at the level of
themintworkshop.com, without copying it. Study: `mint-study.md`. Our
material: `inventory.md`. Three original homepage mocks were built and judged
(`judging.json`, `directions/`).

**Base: `directions/bold-graphic`** (strongest thumb-stopper, real cards shown
as physical white-bordered prints, the hero Mantle slider inside a clear
acrylic one-touch holder, cobalt + sun yellow + ink, Big Shoulders Display +
Schibsted Grotesk). Its tokens and section plan are in `judging.json`.

## Grafts
- From **workshop**: persistent "Book a Restoration" in the mobile header;
  CTA every 1-2 screens; asymmetric results bento with a dark stat block;
  process steps beside a sticky photo; solid action-colour risk panel.
- From **editorial**: studio seamless backdrop / multiply treatment for
  cut-out-on-white photos (Mantle glove pair, Garchomp corner); the Charizard
  case as a ruled "lot sheet" ledger ($400 / PSA 10 / $2,500, no
  strike-through); a DM-screenshot "receipts" strip under one featured pull
  quote instead of three screens of review cards; the oversized footer
  wordmark; the phone hero order (headline, then slider in the first screen,
  then CTAs).
- From bold-graphic itself: oversized step numerals; small card-shape
  separators in the symptoms ticker; Trusted-by nameplates with handle,
  followers and an Instagram tag.

## Must fix (from the judges)
- Slider labels: BEFORE over the damaged side, AFTER over the restored side,
  matching the slider's actual layers; never clipped by the holder.
- Mobile: the holder slider at least half-visible in the first 844px; compact
  sticky Book pill in the header.
- Cobalt must not flood: cobalt, yellow and ink each lead at most one block
  in a row; reviews on chalk/white with cobalt accents.
- Stickers capped at about 2 per screen; none over the headline or holder.
- Keep distance from the competitor's grammar: the symptoms ticker stays
  (owner asked) but flat/quiet, not their tilted tape look.
- Photo repetition: vary crops; the same Mantle shot must not appear 5 times.
- AA contrast for small text on cobalt and yellow; reduced motion static.
- Owner wording verbatim (em dashes included); no new claims; the "Vintage or
  modern" sticker only as a fragment of the owner's FAQ sentence, or dropped.
- Logo: `/card-doctor.jpg` stays the owner's brand mark (PRODUCT.md); an owned
  "CD" monogram is offered to the owner as an option, not swapped in.
- Reels: real reel links/covers will come from the owner (`lib/reels.ts`);
  until then distinct crops and no fake view counts.

## Not allowed
Copying the competitor's name, marks, palette (sage/off-white/near-black/
tomato), fonts (Poppins 800 caps + Yellowtail + Inter Tight), tier names,
prices, copy, photos, or layout signatures listed in `mint-study.md`.
No stock or AI card photos.
